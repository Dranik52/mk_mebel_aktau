# MK Mebel visual system

Real completed furniture leads the page. A split first screen pairs the offer with an unaltered project photograph; the portfolio immediately follows. White and pale green surfaces suit browsing interiors in daylight, while forest green references the photographed kitchen joinery.

Typography: Golos Text with Cyrillic support. Headings use medium weight, close spacing and clear size hierarchy. Body text is at least 16px; secondary text uses dark green-grey rather than low-contrast beige. Functional labels are at least 11px, generally 12–14px.

Brand identity is fixed: retain the original circular dark MK mark, gold lettering, Fraunces wordmark and original brand spacing. Page typography and palette must not restyle the company logo.

Palette: white #ffffff; pale green #f1f4f2; ink #172c25; secondary #4f6058; action #24543f; rules #cdd7d0. No decorative gradients, glass, eyebrow labels, icon-card grids, or artificial illustrations. Gradients only ensure readable captions on photographs.

Layout: 1400px content region, 48px desktop gutters, 20px phone gutters. Services use open rows; prices form one comparison table; process alone retains meaningful sequence numbers. Photography is rectangular. Forms and calculator have restrained rounding. Keyboard focus is visible. Reduced motion disables animation.

Preserve existing section ids, bilingual copy, prices, business claims, contact destinations, portfolio image lists and calculator computation.
